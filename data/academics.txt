1. Overview

The National Institute of Technology Warangal (NITW), established in 1959 as the first Regional Engineering College in India, is a premier public technical university recognized as an Institute of National Importance. The institute is known for its strong academic curriculum, research culture, faculty expertise, and industry-oriented programs. NITW operates on a fully residential campus, creating an immersive academic and research environment.

3. Academic Curriculum

The curriculum follows:

Outcome-Based Education (OBE) model

Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS)

Mandatory internships, seminars, and project work

Frequent curriculum revisions to match industry trends

The flexibility includes:

Elective courses

Open electives across departments

Minor specialization options

Honors programs in B.Tech

4. Teaching & Faculty

Active involvement in research projects, consultancy, and publications

Faculty-student ratio is typically between 1:12 and 1:14

Teaching methods include:

Lectures

Laboratory sessions

Workshops

Seminars

Industry sessions

Project-based learning
