1. Overview

The Training and Placement Cell (TPC) of NIT Warangal is one of the most active and efficient in India. It handles campus placements for B.Tech, M.Tech, MSc, MBA, and PhD students. NITW consistently achieves excellent placement percentages, with strong participation from top national and international companies.

2. Key Highlights

Consistent 90–98% placement rate for B.Tech across branches

Highly competitive salaries with increasing trends every year

Presence of global recruiters from technology, finance, consulting, core engineering, and R&D

Strong alumni network contributing to recruitment and referrals

4. Salary Statistics (Typical Ranges)

(Values vary year to year; these ranges represent common trends at NITW)

A. B.Tech

Highest Package: 40–60 LPA (sometimes higher depending on year)

Average Package: 12–18 LPA

Median Package: 10–13 LPA

Top Branches: CSE, ECE, EEE

B. M.Tech

Highest Package: 25–40 LPA

Average Package: 9–14 LPA

Good opportunities in VLSI, embedded systems, computer science, and core engineering fields

C. MBA

Highest Package: 12–15 LPA

Average Package: 7–10 LPA

D. MSc

Average Package: 6–9 LPA

Roles mainly in analytics, education, research labs, and software

5. Placement Process
A. Pre-Placement

Companies register and share job profiles

Pre-placement talks (PPTs) conducted

Eligibility and criteria shared with students

B. Selection Rounds

Online tests / coding tests

Group discussions (for some roles)

Technical interviews

HR interviews

C. Finalization

Offer letters issued

Students choose between multiple offers (if applicable)

6. Internships

Mandatory internships for B.Tech, M.Tech, and MBA students

Opportunities through:

On-campus internship drives

Research internships at IITs, IISc, foreign universities

Industry training at top companies

Stipends vary from ₹15,000 to ₹1,00,000+ per month depending on company
