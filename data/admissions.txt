1. Overview

Admissions to the National Institute of Technology Warangal (NITW) are conducted through national-level entrance examinations. The institute follows a transparent and centralised admission process for all programs including B.Tech, M.Tech, MSc, MBA, and PhD. Admissions are merit-based and governed by Government of India guidelines.

2. Admission Categories

Undergraduate (B.Tech)

Postgraduate (M.Tech, MSc, MBA)

Doctoral (PhD)

Sponsored / Part-Time Programs

3. B.Tech Admissions
A. Entrance Exam

JEE Main (conducted by NTA)

B. Counselling Process

Admissions through JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority)

Further rounds through CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) if seats remain vacant

C. Eligibility Criteria

Passed 10+2 with PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)

Qualified JEE Main

Category-wise and state-wise seat allocation applies

50% seats reserved for Home State (Telangana) candidates

50% seats for Other States

D. Factors Affecting Admission

JEE Main rank

Category (GEN/EWS/OBC/SC/ST)

Home State quota

Branch-specific cutoffs

Seat availability

4. M.Tech Admissions
A. Entrance Exam

GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering)

B. Counselling Process

Centralised through CCMT (Centralized Counselling for M.Tech)

C. Eligibility Criteria

B.Tech/B.E. degree in relevant field

Valid GATE score

Minimum CGPA/percentage as per department requirement

Relaxation for reserved categories per government norms

D. Special Features

Sponsored seats available

Scholarships available through MHRD for GATE-qualified students

5. MSc Admissions
A. Entrance Exam

IIT JAM (Joint Admission Test for MSc)

B. Counselling Process

Through CCMN (Centralized Counselling for MSc/Int. MSc)

C. Eligible Programs

MSc Physics

MSc Chemistry

MSc Mathematics

6. MBA Admissions
A. Entrance Exams Accepted

CAT

MAT

CMAT

XAT

B. Selection Process

Shortlisting based on entrance exam score

Group Discussion (GD)

Personal Interview (PI)

C. Eligibility

Bachelor’s degree in any discipline

Minimum 60% marks (55% for reserved categories)

7. PhD Admissions
A. Admission Cycles

Twice a year: July and December

B. Selection Process

Written Test

Personal Interview

Evaluation of Research Proposal (in some departments)

C. Eligibility

Master’s degree in relevant field

Good academic record

GATE/NET qualification preferred

Part-time and sponsored category also available

8. Seat Reservation Policy

As per Government of India norms:

GEN-EWS: 10%

OBC-NCL: 27%

SC: 15%

ST: 7.5%

PwD: 5% horizontal across all categories

9. Admission Timeline (General Pattern)
B.Tech

JEE Main exams: January & April

JoSAA counseling: June–July

M.Tech

GATE exam: February

CCMT counseling: April–June

MSc

JAM exam: February

CCMN counseling: April–June

MBA

Applications: January–March

GD/PI: April–May

PhD

Notifications: May & November

Selection: June–July & December

100% online admission process

Strict adherence to national reservation policies

No management quota

Admission purely on merit and ranks in national exams
