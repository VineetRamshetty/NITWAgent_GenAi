1. Overview

The campus of the National Institute of Technology Warangal (NITW) spans approximately 250 acres, offering a fully residential and academically stimulating environment. Located in Hanamkonda, Warangal, Telangana, the campus is known for its greenery, spacious layout, advanced infrastructure, and well-planned academic, residential, and recreational zones.

2. Campus Layout

The campus is divided into the following major zones:

Academic Zone – classrooms, labs, departments, administrative blocks

Residential Zone – student hostels, faculty/staff quarters

Recreational Zone – sports grounds, auditoriums, activity centers

Research and Innovation Zone – specialized labs, centers of excellence

Library and Information Center

Dining and Shopping Area
