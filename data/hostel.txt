1. Overview

NIT Warangal is a fully residential institute, providing separate hostel facilities for boys and girls. The hostels are designed to support comfortable living, academic focus, and community interaction. All hostels are located within the main campus and are monitored for safety, hygiene, and maintenance.

2. Hostel Capacity & Allocation

Multiple hostels for boys and girls, accommodating students from B.Tech, M.Tech, MSc, MBA, and PhD programs.

First-year students are allotted specific hostels near academic blocks.

Senior students stay in branch-specific or year-specific hostels.

Rooms are allotted based on:

Year of study

Availability

Category (UG/PG/PhD)

3. Room Types

Single rooms – usually allocated to final-year UG students, PG students, and research scholars

Double rooms – for most UG students

Triple-sharing rooms – in some first-year hostels

Each room typically includes:

Cot & mattress

Study table & chair

Cupboard

Ceiling fan

Basic lighting

4. Hostel Facilities
A. Essentials

24×7 electricity and water supply

Wi-Fi enabled hostels

Solar water heaters in some hostels

Purified drinking water (RO systems)

Common washrooms and housekeeping staff

B. Common Amenities

Study rooms

Common rooms with TV

Indoor games (carrom, chess, table tennis)

Reading corners

Laundry facilities (manual + paid services)

C. Mess Facilities

Each hostel has its own mess or shares a central mess

Hygienic, nutritious food served on a rotational menu

Veg and non-veg options available

Special meals during festivals and important events

Mess Committees involving students ensure quality control

5. Safety & Security

Dedicated wardens for each hostel

Security guards at all entry/exit points

CCTV installed in corridors and common areas

Strict entry register for visitors

Mandatory hostel timings (especially for girl hostels)

6. Cleanliness & Maintenance

Daily cleaning of common areas

Regular sanitation of washrooms and corridors

Dedicated maintenance staff for electrical and plumbing issues

Complaint management system for quick issue resolution

7. Hostel Environment

Peaceful and student-friendly

Encourages academic focus and peer learning

Frequent hostel-level events, celebrations, and competitions

Students from diverse backgrounds creating an inclusive culture

8. Additional Facilities

Bicycle and vehicle parking areas

Water coolers and geysers

Gardens and open sitting spaces

In-hostel stationery shops or small amenities stores

First-aid kits and medical assistance from Health Centre

9. Hostel Rules

Mandatory attendance during certain timings for first-year students

No ragging; strict anti-ragging policies enforced

Quiet hours during study time

Visitors allowed only in designated areas and timings

Cleanliness and discipline maintained by hostel committees

10. Advantages of Staying in NITW Hostels

Close proximity to academic buildings

Strong peer learning culture

Access to student communities and clubs

Safe environment for all genders

Affordable accommodation and food

Helps students stay connected to campus activities
